nasin Luka Pona

Tap a key for the word in the middle, or flick left, up, right or down for the words around it. Related words share a key, and most words sit in the direction their sitelen pona glyph points. Try it with your finger on a phone.

Switch layers in the left column
Directions follow the glyphs. mi points down, sina points up and ona points left, so you flick that way. pona (∪) is up and ike (∩) is down; lili and suli sit on opposite sides. In the Position key, insa (dot inside) is the tap, sinpin (dot in front) is right, monsi (dot behind) is left, anpa is down. tan (where you come from) is left and tawa (where you go) is right.
Right column: ⌫ tap deletes a letter, a flick in any direction deletes a word · space (the blank key) · punctuation (tap “.”, ← “,”, ↑ “?”, → “!”, ↓ “:”) · ⏎ return. Left column: ABC letters · layer 2 (soweli) · layer 3 (poki) · next keyboard. While layer 2 or 3 is open, its key shows toki and takes you back to layer 1.
Layer 1 holds 57 words that cover about 87% of the words in real toki pona text. Empty slots on layers 2 and 3 are left for new words (nimisin) or your own picks.
